Meet the doctor

Dr. Gloria De La Vara, CAc., DC

Our principal provider is Dr. Gloria De La Vara. She is a licensed chiropractor and certified acupuncturist. Dr. Gloria’s training has focused on blending western and eastern medicine by utilizing orthopedic and neurological evaluations and implementing classic Chinese medicine therapy and treatment.

Techniques incorporated by Dr. Gloria include chiropractic manipulation, soft tissue therapy, rehabilitation with home care exercises geared for reconditioning, nutrition, acupuncture, and sports medicine injury prevention and rehabilitation.

  • 1997 Doctor of Chiropractic, Palmer College of Chiropractic, Davenport, Iowa
  • 2000 Certified in acupuncture; trained at the International Institute of Chinese Medicine, Denver, Colorado
  • B.A. Communication, University of California, San Diego
